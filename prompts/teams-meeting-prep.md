Prepare me for a Teams meeting: $@

1. Run `teams_list_meetings` to find the meeting. If nothing was specified,
   take the next one starting from now. Show me which meeting you picked.

2. Run `teams_get_meeting` for the full details — attendees, agenda, join link.

3. Search Teams for context with `teams_search_messages`: the meeting subject,
   and the names of the attendees. Read the relevant threads with
   `teams_read_thread` or `teams_read_chat`.

4. Give me:
   - **When and who** — time, join link, attendees and their roles if visible
   - **Context** — what has been discussed about this recently, with dates
   - **Open items** — questions left unanswered in the threads
   - **Suggested talking points** — three to five, grounded in what you read

Do not message anyone.
